01 / WHO WE ARE

MORE THAN A TEAM.
A SHARED PASSION.

Welcome to ASLAM GARAGE MOTORS TEAM, a project built around a passion for cars, rally motorsport and the adventures that come with pursuing a dream.

This website is our place to document the journey: the ideas, preparation, challenges, lessons and memorable moments and the history that will shape our progress through the years.

Inspired by the toyota celica of the big brother of my dad, rally number 68, and representing rwanda, we want to share our interest in motorsport and the work that goes into developing our project.

This is only the beginning. As our journey develops, this page will grow with our real experiences, photographs and updates.
